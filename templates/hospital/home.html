Role-Based Access Control

Designed specifically around the three core stakeholder roles

Administrator

Full Control

Manage users, assign roles, maintain departments and doctor profiles, view system-wide revenue, and examine graphical analytics.

  • User role management
  • Interactive analytics charts
  • Full database CRUD access

Doctor / Staff

Partial Control

Doctors review assigned patients, manage daily schedules, confirm or complete appointments, create clinical diagnoses, and issue prescriptions with report attachments.

  • Today's patient schedule
  • Prescriptions & diagnosis
  • Lab report uploads

Patient / User

Basic Access

Patients explore doctors and clinical departments, book appointments, view visit histories, download doctor prescriptions, and track billing invoices.

  • Online appointment booking
  • Prescription & medical history
  • Invoices & digital payments
